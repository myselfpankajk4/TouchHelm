    Private Tailscale HTTPS

    Check Tailscale to begin.

    1. Prepare this PC. Click Set up trusted pairing below. TouchHelm installs missing PC requirements; approve the Windows installer if asked.
    2. Sign in on Windows. Open Tailscale from the system tray, choose Log in and finish in your browser. Keep Tailscale connected.
    3. Connect your phone. Install Tailscale for Android, sign in with the same account (or an authorised account on the same tailnet), then turn its connection on and approve Android’s VPN prompt.
    4. Finish private HTTPS. Click Set up trusted pairing again after login, or Check connection → Enable private HTTPS. If certificate permission is requested, a tailnet administrator must enable HTTPS in the Tailscale admin console.
    5. Scan and pair. Scan the new .ts.net:8443 QR and enter the four-digit host PIN. Keep Tailscale connected on both devices, including when using mobile data. No phone CA certificate is required.

    Private to your tailnet. No public Funnel and no manual CA certificate. Your tailnet may require an administrator to enable HTTPS certificates.
